Responsible AI Usage

Last updated: July 25, 2026

1. Our Commitment

Bluebound Ink is a personal digital space dedicated to creative explorations, books, and music. While we utilize modern technology and AI-assisted tools to prototype and refine our digital experience, we believe in transparency, human curation, and the ethical integration of artificial intelligence.

2. How AI is Utilized

AI tools may be used selectively across our platform to support specific workflows, including:

  • Assisting with code structure, layout prototyping, and backend integrations.
  • Brainstorming design elements and formatting layouts.
  • Assisting in drafting structural outlines or technical documentation.

3. Human Oversight & Curation

Despite the integration of technical tooling, the core creative direction, literary reviews, music selections, and final editorial decisions on Bluebound Ink remain strictly driven by human perspective. Every piece of content published is reviewed, shaped, and maintained with individual intent.

4. Contact

If you have any questions or feedback regarding our use of AI tools on this platform, please feel free to reach out directly.
