The Burning God (The Poppy War, #3)

by R.F. Kuang
★ 4.47 / 5 • Added: Feb 2026
"To save your people, you must first be willing to burn everything down."

R.F. Kuang brings *The Poppy War* trilogy to a devastating, uncompromising conclusion in *The Burning God*. Severely betrayed and stripped of her alliances, Rin must rally the Southern provinces, harness the terrifying power of the Chthonic gods, and fight a multi-front war against both foreign colonizers and domestic tyrants.

Drawing heavily from modern revolutionary history, Kuang explores the tragic trajectories of fanaticism, ideological purity, and the immense human cost of rebellion. Rin's evolution from a desperate orphan into a mythical figure of destruction is heart-wrenching and profound.

A monumental, emotionally harrowing finale that cements the trilogy as a towering achievement in modern dark fantasy.
