The Burning God (The Poppy War, #3)

by R.F. Kuang
★ 4.47 / 5 • Added: Feb 2026
"To save her people, she would have to become the monster they feared."

R.F. Kuang’s trilogy concludes with a devastating, uncompromising finale that cements *The Poppy War* series as a monumental achievement in modern grimdark fantasy.

Following Rin as she navigates the wreckage of betrayal, revolution, and the heavy burden of godhood, the narrative refuses to offer easy redemptive arcs. Every victory is soaked in sacrifice, and the emotional resonance of the characters' fates lingers long after the final page.

A heartbreaking, powerful masterclass in concluding an epic trajectory.
