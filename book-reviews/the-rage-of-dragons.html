The Rage of Dragons (The Burning, #1)

by Evan Winter
★ 4.37 / 5 • Added: Mar 2026
"Vengeance is a fire that consumes everything—starting with the soul of the person who carries it."

Evan Winter launches an absolute adrenaline-fueled masterpiece of African-inspired epic fantasy with *The Rage of Dragons*. In a world locked in a brutal, centuries-long war where the nobility command dragons and commoners are treated as expendable infantry, Tau is a young man stripped of his family, his future, and his rightful place in society.

Refusing to accept a system built on caste oppression, Tau undertakes a suicidal quest for training and vengeance, pushing his mind and body past absolute breaking points. The pacing is relentless, featuring some of the most dynamic, kinetic martial arts and tactical combat choreography ever put to paper.

A blistering, electrifying debut that redefines high-stakes fantasy action and unyielding determination.
