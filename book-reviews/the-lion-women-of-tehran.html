The Lion Women of Tehran

by Marjan Kamali
★ 4.67 / 5 • Added: Jun 2026
#historical-fiction
"True sisterhood is a sanctuary built of courage, resilience, and unyielding loyalty against the tides of history."

Marjan Kamali delivers a sweeping, deeply emotional historical novel set against the turbulent backdrop of mid-20th-century Iran. *The Lion Women of Tehran* explores an enduring bond of female friendship forged in a changing society, tracing how personal lives and ambitions are upended by political upheaval and revolution.

Kamali’s storytelling is rich, vivid, and profoundly empathetic, capturing the vibrant culture of Tehran alongside the heart-wrenching realities of betrayal and survival. The characters are complex and deeply human, anchoring the sweeping historical events in raw, intimate emotion.

A magnificent, resonant tribute to the fierce resilience of women navigating the crucible of history.
