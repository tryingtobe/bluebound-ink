The Jasad Crown (The Scorched Throne, #2)

by Sara Hashem
★ 4.64 / 5 • Added: Mar 2026

"A crown forged from ash and survival holds a weight that no throne can ever soften."

Sara Hashem returns with a brilliant, breathless continuation of the Scorched Throne series in *The Jasad Crown*. Following the explosive revelations and high-stakes dangers of the first book, the narrative deepens into intricate political machinations, shifting loyalties, and the heavy burdens of leadership and heritage.

The chemistry, tension, and strategic maneuvering between characters remain razor-sharp. Hashem masterfully balances rich Middle Eastern-inspired mythology with rapid pacing and deeply emotional character arcs, forcing her protagonists to decide what they are truly willing to sacrifice for freedom.

A riveting, masterfully executed sequel that expands the world and elevates the stakes to magnificent heights.
