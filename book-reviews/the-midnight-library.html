The Midnight Library (reread)

by Matt Haig
★ 3.85 / 5 • Added: Jan 2026
"The only way to learn is to live."

Matt Haig’s comforting and philosophical novel explores the infinite possibilities of regret and choice. Situated between life and death stands the Midnight Library, a place where every book provides an opportunity to try out another life you could have lived if you had made different choices.

Nora Seed navigates through careers she abandoned, relationships she let slip away, and dreams she never pursued, searching for the perfect existence. Revisiting the story offers a gentle reminder about perspective, mental health, and how the ordinary flaws of our current lives are often what make them worth living.

A warm, contemplative fable on gratitude, second chances, and the beauty found in the life we already have.
