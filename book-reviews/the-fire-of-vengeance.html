"When the world is built on a foundation of ash, even the victors are swallowed by the fire."

Evan Winter returns with an explosive, high-octane sequel in *The Fire of Vengeance*. Picking up the pieces after the brutal trials of the first book, Tau's quest for retribution expands from personal survival into a massive, volatile geopolitical conflict that threatens to tear the Omeean empire apart from the inside out.

The scale widens dramatically, introducing terrifying new magical threats, complex political betrayals, and breathless tactical warfare. Winter maintains the relentless pacing and ferocious action that defined the series, while adding deeper layers of emotional consequence to Tau's relentless drive.

A thrilling, masterfully escalated continuation that keeps the adrenaline pumping and the stakes higher than ever.
